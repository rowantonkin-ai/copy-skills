# Cialdini: the influence layer

Assume the books. The seven principles are not re-taught here. What follows is
how each one behaves when the reader is sceptical and has seen every trick,
which is the part that keeps a draft from reading like every other page.

## What this skill owns

`copy-strategist` adds it when a piece needs a compliance trigger built on
purpose. `cashvertising` already carries a compressed version as its principle
10, so reach for this file when the brief needs the depth rather than the
label.

The seven, in one line each. Reciprocity means give first. Commitment means a
small yes makes the next yes easier. Social proof means people follow people
like them. Liking means we say yes to those we warm to. Authority means we
defer to credible expertise. Scarcity means we want what is going away. Unity
means shared identity beats mere liking.

## How each principle lands with a sceptical audience

**Reciprocity works best when the gift is the thing itself.** The standard
play is a lead magnet behind a form. For a relationship-building brand, a
stronger version is to give away a working piece of what you know in the
channel's native format, ungated, with credit to anyone whose idea it builds
on. The gift is the artifact rather than access to it. Test your own brand's
position on gating before a draft reaches for "download the free guide."

**Authority should be earned, not borrowed.** Cialdini's version includes
transferred credibility: titles, logos, lab coats. Readers now discount
borrowed symbols fast. Claim authority where you have operator receipts, and
take a documenter footing ("here is what I tried and what happened") where you
do not. Getting that backwards is the fake-guru tell.

**Social proof has to pass the swap test.** "Join 50,000 marketers" reads fine
and still fails, because deleting your name leaves a sentence any competitor
could have written. Proof that works is specific, sourced, and traceable to a
real record. When there is no receipt, the claim changes shape rather than
getting softened.

**Liking is the human layer.** A person with a face talking to a person with a
name. Personal register, real circumstance, and a visible point of view do
more here than any engineered rapport move.

**Commitment should be rationed.** Small-yes ladders are legitimate, but a
feed full of asks trains readers to scroll past. Decide how many commercial
asks per week or per sequence your channel can carry, and treat each
micro-commitment as spending from that budget.

**Scarcity is the one that most often backfires.** Countdowns, "ends Friday",
and seat counts read as pressure on surfaces where you are building trust.
Soft urgency, stated as posture, usually lands better there. The exception is
a real constraint on a real offer at the bottom of the funnel, which can be
stated plainly because it is true.

**Unity is the strongest available lever and the least used.** Name the shared
identity (practitioner rather than commentator, builder rather than spectator)
and write from inside it. Naming a shared circumstance that was outside
everyone's control invites the reader onto your side.

## The problem with expert audiences

If you write for marketers, salespeople, or anyone who has read *Influence*,
they know these principles by name and can spot an engineered trigger faster
than the copy can land it. Two consequences.

Visible technique costs more than it earns, so the principle should be true
before it is deployed. Real proof rather than manufactured proof, a real
constraint rather than a manufactured deadline.

Never explain how your own audience is being influenced. Demonstrating a
technique the piece is teaching is fine. Narrating the influence stack aimed
at this reader is not.

## A note on checks

Standard Cialdini phrasings usually pass mechanical style checks clean.
Nothing mechanical will stop a generic social-proof line from shipping. The
real constraints are judgment ones: the swap test above, your voice guide's
register rules, and your brand's stance on gating and urgency. Do not read a
clean style check as approval of the trigger.

## For work on other businesses

A client engagement uses Cialdini unmodified. The narrowing above is for
brands building trust with a sceptical audience, and a client selling to a
different buyer may need the scarcity and reciprocity plays exactly as
written.

## Handoff

Name which principles were engineered and where, so a reviewer can judge them.
Flag any trigger that is asserted rather than true.

If you have a voice guide or copy checker, anything drafted here goes through
it before it is handed back.

## Boundaries

It does not verify that a proof point is real, and it makes no ethical
judgment about deploying a trigger, which is the human's call. It cannot
manufacture authority that has not been earned or scarcity that does not
exist.
